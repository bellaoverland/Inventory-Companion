# SL Inventory Companion

**Organize a huge Second Life inventory in minutes, not hours.**
Free Windows app by **Bella Overland Software** — works next to Firestorm.

## Download
👉 **[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)** — open *Releases*, then click the `.exe` file under **Assets**.

Run it, choose where to install, and start it from the desktop shortcut. Log in with your Second Life name and password.

## What it does
- **Find duplicates** — scan one section (e.g. "house") or everything, and move extra copies to Trash.
- **Update many outfits at once** — add or replace an item in dozens of outfits.
- **Gather folders** — collect every folder of a product or creator (e.g. "Paloma", "Layla") into one folder, with all their contents.
- **Search inside all subfolders** — find "Lara", "Maitreya", "Legacy" fitted items anywhere.
- **Send items to friends**, friends list and chat.
- **Your look** — desktop colors, backgrounds, icon and text size.

## Safe by design
- Official Second Life login. Your password is never saved.
- Nothing is deleted — items go to your recoverable Trash.
- Folders move with everything inside them.
- Moves are checked on the Second Life server before they are reported as done.

## Note
Second Life allows one login per account: while the app works on your inventory, Firestorm on the **same** account is logged out.
Accounts with Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) are not supported yet.
